## DEVELOPMENT — `scripts/record.test.js`, 1336 assertions

The site's five-for-five pattern is that its bugs live where it makes a promise in prose. Every
existing suite guards one of those. Nothing guarded the promise the **record** makes, which is the
one the whole site rests on.

Five rules: structure (contiguous day numbers, ascending dates, nothing dated in the future);
sourcing (a named outlet and an absolute https URL, no duplicate URLs, **no aggregator cited as a
source** per §3b); witnesses (name, role, a gist with substance, no repeats); **a day that says
someone testified must list a witness**; and no merge-conflict markers in anything that renders.

Two things are reported rather than failed, because both are legitimate: a day with no witnesses
(procedural days exist) and a single-sourced day (AGENT.md allows one for scheduling). Nine such
notes stand today.


rule threshold was set against that entry rather than guessed.
