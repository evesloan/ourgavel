restates one is just a sixth copy waiting to drift. The suite asserts it can still find both, so if
someone renames `verb`, the suite fails loudly instead of silently checking against nothing. That is
mutation 17, and it was caught.

one run — but had that assertion not existed, the natural fix under time pressure is to loosen the
edge rule, and the suite would have shipped checking nothing at all. **Assert that your test can
still see, not just that it passes.**
