### Diagnosis (tested, not inferred)
- The engine reads only the five feeds configured for the case. Of those, exactly **two** carried
  a Vegas-local verdict headline: 8newsnow (Nexstar) and news3lv (Sinclair). The other three
  (fox5vegas whole-site arc feed, npr 1001, pbs headlines) are broad national feeds that never
  surfaced the specific headline.
- `assess()` needs **3 independent families** (`MIN_OUTLETS=3`) agreeing within a **12h window**
  (`WINDOW_HOURS=12`), then survival of a second cycle. It held at **2/3** — status `watch`, never
  `ready`, so `pending` never armed.
- The three guilty items in the ticker are now aged past the window (news3lv 12.1h & 12.8h,
  8newsnow 18.0h). Verified live: `assess(real ticker, now)` → `{"status":"none"}`, **0 guilty
  families within 12h**. The window has closed. **The engine cannot fire this verdict on its own
  from the current ticker.** This is a genuinely missed verdict, not "settling with runway" as the
  00:27/00:41 runs predicted before the fleet went quiet.

### What I shipped — feed widening (does NOT write the banner; §3b respected)
Added two **verified independent** Vegas newsroom feeds to `duane-davis-tupac/case.json` via an
idempotent script (`scripts/add-tupac-verdict-feeds.js`):
- Las Vegas Review-Journal — `https://www.reviewjournal.com/crime/courts/feed/` (family `reviewjournal`)
- KTNV Las Vegas — `https://www.ktnv.com/news.rss` (family `ktnv`, Scripps)

Both fetched and confirmed this run: valid RSS, each carrying the Davis guilty item, each resolving
to a family distinct from the two already present. Local simulation confirms that with any one 3rd
independent family carrying a fresh (<12h) guilty item, `assess()` returns `ready GUILTY`, arms
`pending`, and fires on the next cycle. This widens coverage 2→4 families so the engine can reach
threshold **on its own** as fresh Sep-1 coverage (sentencing-scheduling, reaction) lands. It does
not, and must not, write the verdict — that is the engine's alone.

### Still needs a decision (flagged to dev + Eve)
