## Defect found and fixed: the ticker was showing readers the same story four times

Not in a queue, so no queue would have caught it. Found by reading what a case page actually renders.

**Symptom, live at `eae7a31`:** the Lil Durk case page — an active trial — rendered its eight-row
"latest" ticker as **two headlines, four times each**. Murdaugh's ticker held 100 items containing
**23 distinct stories**.

| case | items | distinct |
|---|---|---|
| lil-durk-murder-for-hire | 42 | 4 |
| alex-murdaugh-retrial | 100 | 24 |
| lindsay-clancy | 21 | 20 |
| duane-davis-tupac | 23 | 23 |
| mario-fernandez-bridegan | 5 | 5 |

**Cause.** Our Bing News feeds never give us the article. They give us
`bing.com/news/apiclick.aspx?…&tid=<token>&url=<the real article>`, and **`tid` is regenerated on
every request**. `pollCase()` dedupes with `hash(it.link)`, so the same story was a brand-new item
every fifteen minutes, forever. 142 of 191 ticker items across the site (74%) were such links; two
cases were 100% affected.

**Three consequences, in increasing order of how much they matter:**

1. The page looks broken, on the case with the most current attention.
2. **The ticker is capped at 100 items, so real headlines were being evicted by copies of
   themselves.** That is silent loss of record.
3. `T.seen` is capped at 600 ids, and the redirector churn generated ~140 fresh ids per poll — so
   the churn destroyed dedupe memory for the *healthy* feeds too. That is how two copies of one
   Court TV story reached the Clancy ticker from a URL that never changed.

**Also fixed in passing: readers were being sent through a click-tracker.** Every citation on a
murder-trial page routed the reader through `bing.com/news/apiclick.aspx` before reaching the
newsroom. That is the same objection SECURITY.md already records against hotlinked images and social
embeds — a third party learns who read which murder-trial page — and it gets the same answer:
deep-link to the outlet, make the destination visible in the href, add no third party.

The links were **not** broken, which was worth checking rather than assuming: the feed's `&amp;`
entities leave the query parsing as `amp;url=`, with no `url` param at all, but Bing tolerates it and
still 302s to the right article (verified by fetching one).

### The fix

`scripts/canonical.js` — new, no dependencies:

- `resolveUrl(link)` — what the reader is sent to. Decodes XML entities, unwraps *known* redirectors
  (host-suffix matched, so `bing.com.evil.example` does not qualify), strips tracking params, drops
  the fragment. **Anything unrecognised comes back byte-identical.**
- `itemKey(link)` — dedupe identity. Normalises harder (case, trailing slash, sorted query) because
  it is never rendered.
- `dedupeItems(items)` — first occurrence wins, order preserved, idempotent.

`scripts/poll.js`, five lines: hash the article identity instead of the feed's link; store the
resolved URL; check the durable stored list as well as the capped `seen` set; and **canonicalise and
collapse the existing list on every run**, so the cases that already accumulated copies converge on
the next pulse rather than needing a data patch. The ticker is now sorted by timestamp before
slicing — with the duplicates gone, a mis-ordered row is plainly visible in a four-row list.

**No data files are touched.** The pulse commits `data` before it builds, and the heal is in
`poll.js`, which is where persisted ledgers have to be written.

### `scripts/canonical.test.js` — new, 59 assertions

Two halves on purpose, the same discipline as `screen.test.js`'s must-publish half: **must unwrap**
(redirectors resolve, rotated tokens collapse to one identity) and **must not touch** (ordinary
newsroom URLs, real query params, `bing.com/news/search`, garbage, and non-http schemes come back
unchanged). A URL canonicaliser that quietly mangles links would be worse than the bug.

It also asserts against **the live record rather than fixtures**, the same reason
`verdict.live-check.js` exists: for every case, no duplicate identities survive, no reader is left
pointed at a click-tracker, no `&amp;` survives into an href, and **the eight rows a case page
renders are eight distinct stories** — the reader-visible symptom, asserted directly.

The six wiring assertions were mutation-tested: reverting `poll.js` fails exactly those five that
should fail, and no others.

**Verified against a fresh clone of `main` at `eae7a31`**, not a working copy: applies clean, builds
34 pages, and `canonical.test.js`, `composer.test.js`, `media-fetch.test.js`, `media.test.js`,
`media.viewer-test.js`, `submit.test.js`, `verdict.test.js`, `audit.js` and `verdict.live-check.js`
all pass. Rendered check before/after: Durk goes from two headlines repeated four times to four
distinct stories; Murdaugh from a repeating handful to eight distinct rulings in date order.
